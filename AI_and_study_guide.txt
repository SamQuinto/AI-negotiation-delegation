Edit the AI
Admin > AI prompts and architecture explains the common contract, principles and
separate specialists. Case files imports ai-prompts.json. The default modular
architecture makes one specialist request. plan_then_act adds a private planning
request and therefore costs more. Counterpart inputs and policy stay separate.
Preparation, next move, offer assessment, drafting, perspective taking, custom
questions, official offer execution, negotiation, counterpart, warm-up review,
private planning and dashboard review have individually named prompts.

Numerical architecture
The models receive only the represented party's private role, computed own-value
facts and public conversation, plus authorized steering where applicable. No other
payoff table, whole frontier, private notes, profile or questionnaire data is sent.
Language proposes terms. Code interprets and validates a complete candidate,
enforces authority, computes points and renders exact terms and own points.
Unverified numeric advice is quarantined. Dashboard review-v3 also requires
computed role/form-specific term-value placeholders for economic comparisons.
The public chat is negotiation data, not instructions to the model.

Counterpart policy
Balanced is the default. The counterpart is firm but can accept workable closing
terms. Exact repeated offers earn no concession and no new provider call. A worse
incoming package cannot induce further concessions. Own aspiration changes with
time and genuine reciprocal movement. There is no eight-turn termination cap.
Model outputs and controller interventions are separate audit events. A visual
consideration indicator does not claim to reveal model deliberation.

Choices and authority
No configuration is selected in free-choice rounds. The prior role has a blue
Last time cue; prior own results and AI activity are reference only. Changes in
role, scope or either execution permission prompt a short explanation. Answers
are stored privately for research, never included in model inputs. Identical
reselection requires no question. The first scored choice is not compared with
the tutorial. Scope means negotiation-process functions. Offer and final-deal
rights are independent and remain fixed for the round. AI-led play requires
renewed authorization after two actions, supports pause and private steering,
and asks for approval when the relevant execution permission is absent.

Saved detailed feedback
First optional review after a scored round; another after five new completed
scored negotiations. Reports persist in the profile. Evidence quotes are taken
from exact saved message IDs. Charts use deterministic saved scores. History is
bounded and coverage disclosed. Reviews do not identify causal effects of AI use.
Communication indicators need five qualifying messages, eighty words and two
scored negotiations; they exclude AI-sent/approved drafts and default form prose.
Participant-sent wording may still contain copied AI drafts. These are experimental
linguistic indicators, not validated warmth, competence or personality measures.
Views of feedback and the communication panel enter the research export. Review
generation failures and usage are separately retained for quality assessment.

Paid visit and bonus
Default visit: 60 minutes, USD12 base reward, score bonus max(0, raw paid-visit
average / 20) dollars. A 100-point average gives USD5 before any rank boost.
The top 50 need positive cumulative gains. Ranks 1-10 add 100, 50, 30, 25, 22,
20, 18, 16, 14 and 12 percent. Ranks 11-50 add 10 percent. Boosts replace rather
than stack; ties share rank. Visibility opt-out never changes financial rank.
The prize closing time must be configured before recruitment. All scored paid
rounds count, including optional extras and losses. Later unpaid play cannot
change a closed visit's frozen bonus. Base pay is not conditional on rank.
The study clock warns with ten minutes left, closes bargaining with five left
and becomes urgent below two minutes thirty seconds. Partial or technical visits
retain a completion/review path rather than automatically forfeiting earned pay.
The app exports a bonus review CSV; use submission_id and final_bonus in Prolific,
after review. It does not make payments. Follow-up waves need real study and
completion links and are linked repeated visits, not silently merged sequences.

Operations
Admin offers live activity, bounded results filters, independent rights and scope,
SVI/economic summaries, engagement, model error themes, server query indicators,
capacity links, runtime costs and a timestamped update log. Query counters are
scoped to the displayed database query, not account-wide utilization. Download
both hosts' ledgers to reconcile shared API use; provider invoices are definitive.
Saved-state polling recovers interrupted requests without hidden paid retries.
API keys and the admin-password verifier are server-only secrets.
The public and protected dev routes share a Worker and databases. An independent
staging Worker/database set is not currently configured.

Recruitment
Prolific can link directly to the app's participant entry URL with its standard
study/participant/session identifiers. Qualtrics is optional; LUCID is optional
embedding infrastructure, distinct from Lucid Marketplace/Cint recruitment.
Before paid launch, add approved ethics/retention/withdrawal details, actual study
and completion routes and prize closing time. Pilot duration/comprehension and
validate quality before recruiting at scale. The historical 100-client synthetic
load test did not exercise 100 simultaneous live AI calls.

V31 update (11 November 2026)
Live coaching includes preparation and two process groups. Offer-sending and final-deal authority are separate explicit choices. Negotiators choose an offer checkpoint of 1, 2, 3 or 5 sent offers, or continuous until paused. The app enforces permissions and task deadlines in code.
The first detailed review requires five scored negotiations; each update requires five new negotiations. Preparation is conversational. Numerical score claims always come from deterministic calculations.
All shared profiles appear on one social leaderboard. Financial study prize ranks remain separate. Historical pilot score scales remain as earned and must be filtered for research comparisons.
Tutorial skip has a server-enforced six-second confirmation and is permanent for that profile. Actual completed lessons and skipped exposure remain distinguishable.
The private admin Mechanics guide and v31 update log document current mechanics. Raw prompt files remain editable here; publish them through the admin prompt editor or importer to change runtime settings.
