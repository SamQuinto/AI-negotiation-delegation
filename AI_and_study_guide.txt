Negotiation Arena v33: operating and analysis notes

AI architecture
The low-cost runtime uses Luna with low reasoning for helper and counterpart. Each receives its own role material and a bounded set of own-value comparisons. It does not receive the other payoff table, exact ZOPA or the joint landscape. Calculation and package validation are deterministic.
Support scope and offer/finalization authority are separate. Three context-specific coaching presets reflect the selected scope. Custom wording cannot expand it. Advice is concise, with a separate public-ready draft when useful. Put in chat, Review as offer and official AI-send actions preserve distinct provenance.
Collaborative, balanced and firm counterpart approaches vary own-value aspirations while answering genuine questions and sharing qualitative priorities. Repeated offers do not earn new concessions. Admin tough raises the target within each approach. Raw responses, corrections and usage are kept in private release evidence. Small probes are not a reliability estimate.

Feedback
The review systematically checks eighteen areas: priority questions, reasons, concessions without a clear return, repetition, package exploration, opening targets, impasse, negative deals, deadlines, responsiveness, hostile wording, warmth, steering, actual coach use, delegation changes, same-case comparisons, evidence sufficiency and complete-score checks.
Observed recent/repeated patterns are ranked by practical impact and theme diversity. The server attaches observations and original evidence IDs. The model writes warm short steps and useful example wording. Invented numerical claims and changed priority IDs are rejected. Unsupported ideas are labelled experiments.
The dashboard puts Top 3 improvements and AI-use tips above historical performance. A first review needs five completed scored rounds; another needs five new rounds. Outdated methods unlock a free replacement without extra rounds, then the usual gate resumes. Current method, format, prompt, planning, model and reasoning fingerprints determine freshness. Old reports remain saved and flagged. Red review-date lines are reconstructed on historical graphs without rewriting scores.
Private notes, profile/recruitment information and survey answers are excluded from review model input. Warmth/competence plots are experimental wording signals, not validated ability or personality scores.

Research
Fixed opening cases standardize exposure but confound case/task with position within a mixed sequence. Use the between-person task assignment for a causal task-type comparison, and treat chosen delegation and feedback exposure as observational. Model individual economic payoff and SVI continuously; any four-pathway display needs prespecified reference thresholds. Scheduled SVI on rounds one, three and five is planned missingness. Optional continued/return play is a separate behavioral outcome.
Equal payoff bounds do not establish equal task difficulty. Retain case, role, opening side, counterpart style, scope, both permissions, cadence, actual helper actions, prompt/scoring/interaction version and whether a review was generated/read/replaced. Freeze the intended research release before recruitment.

Quality and administration
The private admin update log distinguishes UX, Visual, Content, Technical and Mechanical issues with a correction and verification scope. The comprehensive workspace checklist retains 450 requests. Final local regression, type/build, responsive screenshots, exact case audits and deployment receipts are private artifacts.
Phone/tablet/desktop viewport checks are not physical-device testing or full accessibility certification. Local synthetic load tests do not establish a hundred simultaneous real-model participants. Historical known API cost estimates and runtime ledgers are reconciled by response ID; invoices remain authoritative and unpriced/unknown historical calls are disclosed.
Study consent/protocol/payment configuration must be complete before paid recruitment is enabled. Optional unpaid return play does not change a closed paid-visit bonus. Never put keys, participant data or raw private transcripts in this public configuration repository.
